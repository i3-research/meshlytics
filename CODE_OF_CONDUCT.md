# Code of conduct

Meshlytics is a research software project. We want everyone who uses it or contributes to it to be treated with
respect, whatever their background, experience, identity or field.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification when something is unclear.
- Respect people's time, privacy and differing views.
- Give credit for others' work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory or demeaning language or images.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information (including e-mail addresses or data) without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and to anyone representing the project.
Report problems privately to the maintainer, Ankush Kesri, at Ankush.Kesri@childrens.harvard.edu. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or block participants, that break this
code.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
